06 / RESIDENTIAL COMMUNITIES

INDIVIDUAL HOMES. ONE SYSTEM.

Real estate
that generates energy.

Solar rooftops, home backup and central storage form one managed energy system for a residential community.

INVESTMENT MODEL

0CAPEX

Zero upfront
capital expenditure.

Financing, system scope and commercial terms are agreed for each project.

  1. 01 / GENERATION

    Solar rooftops

    Panels on homes generate energy without a separate ground-mounted array.

  2. 02 / HOME BACKUP

    Home storage

    A hybrid inverter and battery support each home’s energy system.

  3. 03 / SHARED SYSTEM

    Central storage

    A second layer of storage works alongside the community’s generation and consumption.

  4. 04 / MANAGEMENT

    Smart metering and control

    One system coordinates energy flows and electric vehicle charging schedules.

Land for living.Land remains available for homes, parks and shared infrastructure.

Home backup comes first.The shared system uses only the capacity authorized by the homeowner.

Energy infrastructure and service.Infrastructure is integrated into the residential development and supported throughout operation.
